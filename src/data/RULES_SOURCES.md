### Encoded from Ch.8 / appendix 1 — not guessed

- Xcel cannot satisfy Development Program mobility.  
- Live sanctioned meets only. Entry from a DP level requires AA at ≥1 sanctioned meet at that DP level.  
- Bronze min age 5 / none; Silver 6 / none; Gold 7 / none; Platinum 8 / **32.00 AA at Gold OR 8.5 IES**; Diamond 9 / **32.00 AA at Platinum OR 8.5 IES**; Sapphire 12 / **32.00 AA at Diamond OR 8.5 IES**.  
- VP/SV: Bronze 4A SV10; Silver 5A SV10; Gold 6A SV10; Platinum 6A+1B SV10; Diamond 5A+2B SV10; Sapphire 3A+3B+1C SV 9.6 + up to 0.40 bonus.  
- Missing SR −0.50 off SV. Restricted skill = no VP, −0.50 off SV.  
- DP cast-angle deductions are **not** used in any Xcel division.  
- Platinum/Diamond floor and Diamond beam dance leap: chart **highlights 155°** and **strikes 150°** (yellow = current; strikethrough = deleted). Do not encode 150°.  
- Dive roll does not fulfill flight SR. Bronze/Silver: RO rebound backward roll is an acro connection.  
- SR1/SR2 may not be the same pass (Bronze–Platinum).  
- Gold+ vault **lists** are in **Xcel Appendix 2** (Aug 2025 values), now local at `docs/usag/appendix2.pdf`. Encoded 2026-09-15 from the chart image (black cell = not allowed). Not guessed.  
- IES (R&P Ch.7 V.B): athletes who compete three events or less. Charts write **8.5 IES** with no further formula.  
- Floor time limits from appendix 1: Bronze 45s; Silver **1:00** (45s struck); Gold 1:00; Platinum **1:30** (1:15 struck); Diamond/Sapphire 1:30.  
- Split SR/VP credit: within 20° of the specified angle (Bronze–Diamond floor / Silver–Diamond beam) or 45° (Sapphire). Deduction up to 0.20 for insufficient split.  
- Long hang pullover is a 360° circling skill only if preceded by a cast — Platinum & Diamond (highlighted).
